Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to rent a car?

A valid driver’s license, a credit/debit card in your name, and you must be at least 21 years old.

Is there a deposit?

Yes, a refundable hold is placed on your card at pickup. The amount depends on vehicle class.

What’s the fuel policy?

Full-to-full. Return the vehicle with the same fuel level to avoid refuel charges.

Can I add an additional driver?

Absolutely. Add during booking or at the counter. A small daily fee may apply.

What if I’m late?

There’s a grace period of 30 minutes. After that, hourly charges apply up to the daily rate.
